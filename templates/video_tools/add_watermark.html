How to Add a Watermark to a Video Online

Protect your videos by adding a custom image watermark using our Add Watermark to Video tool. Watermarks help protect your copyright, promote your brand, and improve visibility across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and other platforms.

Steps to Add a Watermark

  • Upload your video in MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, or WEBM format.
  • Upload your watermark image in PNG, JPG, or JPEG format.
  • Use a transparent PNG logo for the best quality.
  • Choose watermark position, size, and transparency.
  • Click Add Watermark and wait for processing.
  • Preview and download your processed video.

Best Practices

  • Use a transparent PNG logo for professional results.
  • Place watermark in corners to avoid blocking content.
  • Use balanced size and transparency.
  • Maintain consistent branding across videos.
