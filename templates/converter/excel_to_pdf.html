Frequently Asked Questions

Will my Excel formatting be preserved?

The conversion engine aims to preserve standard formatting such as fonts, colors, and basic layouts where supported.

Can I convert an XLSX file to PDF?

Yes, the tool is specifically designed to accept and process standard .xlsx spreadsheet files.

What happens to multiple worksheets?

If the workbook contains multiple sheets with data, they will be sequentially converted into pages of the resulting PDF where supported by the converter.

Will charts and tables appear in the PDF?

Standard tables, grids, and basic charts are designed to be preserved in the output PDF as closely as the conversion engine supports.
