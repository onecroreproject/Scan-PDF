Frequently Asked Questions

What is a QR code?
A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information such as a website URL, text, contact details, phone numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, or location data. A smartphone camera or QR scanner can read the code and open the encoded information.
Is the QR Code Generator free?
The QR Generator can be used to create supported QR code types directly from the ScanPDF website. Users can customize and download the generated QR code using the available design and output options.
What QR code types can I create?
The QR Generator supports URL, Text, Email, Phone, SMS, vCard, Wi-Fi, and Location.
Can I customize the QR code design?
Yes. You can customize foreground and background colors, gradients, logo images, QR body shapes, finder eye frames, and eye ball styles.
Can I add a logo to my QR code?
Yes. Use the Add Logo Image section to upload a logo and place it inside the QR code. Always verify the completed code using a QR scanner after applying a logo.
Why is my QR code not scanning?
Make sure the foreground and background have strong contrast, avoid overly complex styling, keep sufficient empty space around the QR code, and confirm that the encoded data is valid. Test the QR code using a real phone before publishing or printing it.
Which QR format should I download?
PNG or JPG/JPEG is convenient for websites and general digital use. SVG is recommended when available for scalable graphics or professional printing because it can be resized without losing quality.
Do customized QR codes still scan?
Yes, provided the design maintains sufficient contrast and does not interfere with important QR finder patterns. Always scan-test heavily customized QR codes before using them publicly.
Can I create a Wi-Fi QR code?
Yes. Select the Wi-Fi QR type and enter the required network information. Scanning the resulting QR code can help supported devices connect using the encoded Wi-Fi details.
Can I create a vCard QR code?
Yes. The vCard option allows contact details to be encoded into a QR code, making it easier for users to save contact information after scanning.
