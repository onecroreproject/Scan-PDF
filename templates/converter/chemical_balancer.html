Chemical Equation Types

Explore common chemical reactions and balance them instantly.

1

Combination / Synthesis

Two or more substances combine to form one product.

Unbalanced H2 + O2 = H2O
Balanced 2H2 + O2 = 2H2O
2

Decomposition

One compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances.

Unbalanced H2O = H2 + O2
Balanced 2H2O = 2H2 + O2
3

Single Displacement

One element replaces another element in a compound.

Unbalanced Zn + HCl = ZnCl2 + H2
Balanced Zn + 2HCl = ZnCl2 + H2
4

Double Displacement

Parts of two compounds swap places to form two new compounds.

Unbalanced AgNO3 + NaCl = AgCl + NaNO3
Balanced AgNO3 + NaCl = AgCl + NaNO3
5

Combustion

A substance reacts with oxygen, releasing energy (often forming CO2 and H2O).

Unbalanced CH4 + O2 = CO2 + H2O
Balanced CH4 + 2O2 = CO2 + 2H2O
6

Acid + Base / Neutralization

An acid and a base react to form salt and water.

Unbalanced H2SO4 + NaOH = Na2SO4 + H2O
Balanced H2SO4 + 2NaOH = Na2SO4 + 2H2O
7

Redox

A reaction involving the transfer of electrons (oxidation and reduction).

Unbalanced Fe + O2 = Fe2O3
Balanced 4Fe + 3O2 = 2Fe2O3
8

Metal + Acid

A metal reacts with an acid to produce a salt and hydrogen gas.

Unbalanced Mg + HCl = MgCl2 + H2
Balanced Mg + 2HCl = MgCl2 + H2
9

Metal + Water

A metal reacts with water to produce a metal hydroxide and hydrogen gas.

Unbalanced Na + H2O = NaOH + H2
Balanced 2Na + 2H2O = 2NaOH + H2
10

Precipitation

Two soluble salts react to form an insoluble solid (precipitate).

Unbalanced BaCl2 + Na2SO4 = BaSO4 + NaCl
Balanced BaCl2 + Na2SO4 = BaSO4 + 2NaCl
