Online Video Trimmer

Quickly remove unwanted parts from any video. No software needed — just upload, set the time range, and download.

How to Trim a Video

Upload a video, enter the start time and end time in seconds, then click Trim Video. The selected portion will be ready to download.

What Is Video Trimming?

Trimming keeps only the part you need and removes the rest. The original file stays untouched — a new clean file is created.

Choose Start & End Time

Start time is where the kept section begins. End time is where it finishes. End must be greater than start and within the video length.

Who Can Use It?

Students, teachers, content creators, marketers, and anyone who needs a quick cut without heavy editing software.

Supported Formats

MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WEBM, FLV, WMV, M4V and more. Unsupported files are rejected with a clear message.

Why Use an Online Trimmer?

When you only need to cut a short section, a full editor is overkill. Upload → set times → process → download. Fast and simple.

💡 Tip: For best results, use accurate start and end times and prefer MP4 files with moderate resolution.
