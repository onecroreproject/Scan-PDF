Online Video Trimmer

Our online Video Trimmer allows you to quickly remove unwanted portions from a video by selecting a start time and an end time. Instead of using complicated video editing software, you can upload your video, enter the section you want to keep, and generate a trimmed video. This tool is useful for preparing videos for social media, websites, presentations, projects, tutorials, and other digital content.

How to Trim a Video

To trim your video, first upload a supported video file. Enter the starting point in seconds and then enter the ending point in seconds. For example, if you want to keep a section beginning at 10 seconds and ending at 30 seconds, enter 10 as the start time and 30 as the end time. Click the Trim Video button to process the selected portion. After processing is completed, the trimmed video will be available on the result page.

What Is Video Trimming?

Video trimming means removing unwanted sections from the beginning, middle, or end of a video. It allows you to keep only the portion that is useful for your purpose. Video trimming is different from changing the entire video because the original uploaded file is not modified. A new output video is created containing the selected portion.

Choose Start and End Time

The start time determines where the trimmed section begins, while the end time determines where it finishes. The end time must always be greater than the start time. The selected time should also be within the duration of the uploaded video. For example, for a 60-second video, you can select a section from 15 seconds to 45 seconds. Invalid time values are rejected before video processing begins.

Who Can Use the Video Trimmer?

This video trimming tool can be useful for students, teachers, content creators, developers, video editors, businesses, and general users. Students can trim recordings for assignments and presentations. Content creators can remove unnecessary sections before publishing videos. Businesses can shorten demonstrations, tutorials, and promotional videos.

Supported Video Formats

The tool supports commonly used video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, FLV, WMV, and M4V. The uploaded file is validated before processing. Unsupported files, excessively large files, and invalid time values are rejected with a clear error message.

Why Use an Online Video Trimmer?

A simple video trimming tool can save time when you only need to remove unwanted portions of a recording. You do not need to open a full video editing application for a basic trimming operation. Upload the video, specify the required time range, process the file, and download the resulting video.

Important Information

The original video is used as the source for processing and the selected section is generated as a separate output file. For best results, provide accurate start and end times and use a valid video file. If the selected time range is outside the available video duration, the system will show an appropriate validation message instead of processing an invalid request.
