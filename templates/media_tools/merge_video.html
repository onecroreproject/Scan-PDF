How to Merge Videos

Easily combine multiple clips into one complete video. Perfect for social media, presentations, travel videos, and more.

Upload Multiple Videos

Select at least two video files. You can upload clips from your phone, camera, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or any supported source.

Supported Formats

Works with MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WEBM and other common formats. Files are validated before processing begins.

Order Matters

Videos are merged in the exact order you select them. Arrange your clips carefully before uploading for the best result.

Processing Time

Time depends on file size, duration, resolution, and number of videos. Short clips finish quickly; longer ones may take several minutes.

Do Not Close the Page

Please wait until processing is complete. Do not refresh or close the browser while the videos are being merged.

Download Result

After successful processing you will be redirected to the result page where you can preview and download the final merged video.

💡 Tip: For faster results, use MP4 videos with moderate resolution. Upload the clips in the order you want them to appear in the final video.
