How to Use
2
Upload a Video

Click Choose Video and select a supported video file.

  • MP4
  • AVI
  • MOV
  • MKV
  • WEBM
3
Upload an Audio File

Click Choose Audio and select the audio file you want to add.

  • MP3
  • WAV
  • AAC
  • M4A
  • OGG
  • FLAC
4
Select the Processing Mode

Choose one of the available options:

  • Replace Audio — Removes the video's original audio. Uses only the uploaded audio.
  • Merge Audio — Mixes the uploaded audio with the video's original audio.
5
Configure Optional Settings

You can customise the output by:

  • Adjusting the audio volume
  • Enabling audio looping if the audio is shorter than the video

If these settings are not required, the default values can be used.

6
Process the Video

Click the Process Video button. The application will:

  • Validate the uploaded files
  • Read the video information
  • Process the audio
  • Apply the selected settings
  • Replace or merge the audio
  • Generate the final output video
7
Preview the Result

After processing is complete, the output page displays:

  • The processed video
  • Processing status
  • Video preview

You can play the video to verify the result.

8
Download the Video

Click the Download Video button to save the processed video to your local computer.
